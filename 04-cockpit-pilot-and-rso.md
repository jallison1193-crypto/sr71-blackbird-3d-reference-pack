## 5. Cockpit Modeling & Color/Material Guide for Blender

* **Interior Structural Color:** Dark neutral gray (`FS 36231` Dark Gull Gray) tub bulkheads and canopy frames, contrasted against **matte black (`FS 37038`)** instrument bezels and sub-panels.
* **Ejection Seats (Lockheed C-2 / Weber SR-1 Zero-Zero):** Gloss/semi-gloss black structural frame, **bright orange-red cushions and headrest**, silver/steel harness hardware, yellow-and-black striped D-ring ejection pull handle between the knees, and **zinc-chromate gold hydraulic canopy actuator cylinder** behind the seat.
* **Signature SR-71 Instruments to Feature in Close-Up Camera Moves:**
  1. **Triple Display Indicator (TDI):** Digital drum counter + analog strip top-left of center panel showing `KEAS`, `Mach` (past `3.2+`), and `Altitude` (past `85,000 ft`).
  2. **Inlet Spike & Forward Bypass Position Indicators:** Dual-needle gauges monitoring left/right spike translation (`26 in` travel) and shockwave duct pressure ratio (`unstart` prevention).
  3. **Periscope / Optical Viewfinder:** Center-top of Pilot's glare shield (gives rearward/horizon view) and center of RSO's panel (optical navigation/sensor tracking).
  4. **Master Caution Matrix:** Large illuminated amber/red grid on the right forward panel (infamous for requiring two hands to press reset).
